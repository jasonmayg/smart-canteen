Contract

How it works

  1. A bank gives the platform's trading company a credit line.
  2. The caterer uploads its signed school contract and pays a deposit.
  3. The platform sizes a limit from the contract value and deposit.
  4. The trading company buys ingredients in bulk, paying suppliers cash for better prices.
  5. Ingredients reach the kitchen in weekly batches.
  6. As parents' meal fees clear, a fixed share is split off automatically to repay.
